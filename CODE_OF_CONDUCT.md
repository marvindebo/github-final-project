# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, providing constructive feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and prioritizing the well-being of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing another person's private information without permission, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other community interactions. It also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainer through an appropriate GitHub contact channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent possible. Maintainers will determine appropriate corrective action.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the nature, severity, and circumstances of reported conduct when determining a response. Actions may include:

1. Correction: A private explanation of inappropriate behavior and a request for improvement.
2. Warning: A formal warning with expectations for future conduct.
3. Temporary Ban: Temporary suspension from project participation.
4. Permanent Ban: Permanent removal from project participation for severe or repeated violations.

## Attribution

This project adopts these community standards to support respectful and constructive open-source collaboration.
